## Summary

This review examines how peptide-based drugs can overcome chemoresistance in lung cancer, one of the major limitations of conventional platinum-based chemotherapy. It covers anticancer peptides (ACPs) as a therapeutic class with high selectivity, reduced toxicity, and lower propensity to induce drug resistance compared to traditional chemotherapeutics. The paper positions ACPs as a promising near-future treatment approach for lung cancer.

## Background & Motivation

- Lung cancer is a leading global health burden with inadequate treatment options
- Standard chemotherapy (cisplatin + taxanes like paclitaxel/docetaxel) suffers from:
  - Severe long-term adverse effects
  - Acquired drug resistance limiting efficacy
- Chemoresistance mechanisms include:
  - **P-glycoprotein (P-gp) overexpression** — drug efflux pumps reducing intracellular drug concentration
  - **Enhanced DNA repair** — cancer cells repair chemotherapy-induced damage
  - **Cancer stem cell development** — resistant subpopulations survive treatment
  - **Apoptosis dysregulation** — evading programmed cell death
  - **Tumor microenvironment alterations** — stromal support for resistant cells

## Anticancer Peptides (ACPs) — Key Properties

| Property | ACPs | Antibodies |
|----------|------|------------|
| Size | 10-60 amino acids | ~150 kDa proteins |
| Tissue penetration | Superior | Limited |
| Cell internalization | High efficiency | Lower |
| Target affinity | Moderate (weaker than antibodies) | High |
| Half-life | Shorter | Longer |
| Immunogenicity | Low | Higher |
| Drug resistance | Less likely | Can develop resistance |
| Selectivity | High | High |

## How Peptides Overcome Chemoresistance

1. **Membrane disruption** — ACPs can directly lyse cancer cells via membrane interaction, bypassing traditional resistance mechanisms (efflux pumps, DNA repair) entirely
2. **Multi-target action** — peptides targeting multiple pathways simultaneously reduce the likelihood of resistance development
3. **Enhanced drug delivery** — peptide-drug conjugates improve intracellular delivery of chemotherapeutics, overcoming P-gp-mediated efflux
4. **Apoptosis restoration** — pro-apoptotic peptides can re-engage cell death pathways that resistant cells have silenced
5. **Tumor penetration** — superior tissue penetration compared to antibody-based approaches reaches resistant tumor cores

## Significance

- Addresses the critical unmet need of chemoresistance in lung cancer
- Provides a framework for understanding how peptides circumvent classical drug resistance mechanisms
- Highlights ACPs as a bridge between small molecules and biologics — combining advantages of both
- Reviews the therapeutic pipeline of peptide-based drugs with potential for lung cancer

## Limitations

- Full text behind Springer paywall; detailed peptide names/sequences and clinical data not accessible from abstract
- Review article — no new experimental data
- Short half-life and moderate affinity of peptides remain practical challenges for clinical translation
