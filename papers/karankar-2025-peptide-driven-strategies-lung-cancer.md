## Summary

This review provides a comprehensive overview of peptide-based therapeutic strategies currently in use or under investigation for lung cancer treatment. It highlights how peptides can target key oncogenic drivers in NSCLC — including EGFR, TP53, BRAF, MET, ROS1, and ALK — while also covering anti-inflammatory and apoptosis-inducing peptides. The paper positions peptides as targeted and safer alternatives to conventional chemotherapy, which suffers from severe side effects and drug resistance.

## Background & Motivation

- Lung cancer accounts for ~18% of all cancer-related deaths globally
- Conventional treatments (chemotherapy, radiation) have significant limitations: severe side effects, acquired drug resistance
- Molecularly targeted therapies (TKIs) are effective but limited to specific mutation profiles
- Peptides offer advantages: high selectivity, small size, biocompatibility, ease of modification, lower immunogenicity

## Key Molecular Targets Discussed

| Target | Role in Lung Cancer | Peptide Strategy |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| **EGFR** | Overexpressed/mutated in ~15-30% of NSCLC; drives proliferation | EGFR-binding peptides (e.g., GE11), EGFR dimerization inhibitors |
| **TP53** | Tumor suppressor; mutated in ~50% of NSCLC | Peptides restoring p53 function or targeting mutant p53 (e.g., p28/azurin-derived) |
| **BRAF** | Oncogenic kinase; V600E mutation in ~2% of NSCLC | Peptide inhibitors of BRAF signaling |
| **MET** | Receptor tyrosine kinase; amplified in resistant NSCLC | MET-targeting peptides for drug delivery |
| **ROS1** | Receptor tyrosine kinase; fusions in ~1-2% of NSCLC | Targeting peptides for ROS1-rearranged tumors |
| **ALK** | Receptor tyrosine kinase; fusions in ~5% of NSCLC | ALK-targeted peptide strategies |

## Peptide Classifications

The review categorizes lung cancer peptides into two major functional classes:

1. **Anti-NSCLC targeting peptides** — bind to specific oncoproteins (EGFR, ALK, MET, etc.) to block signaling or deliver cytotoxic payloads
2. **Anti-inflammatory and apoptosis-inducing peptides** — directly kill cancer cells via membrane disruption, apoptosis induction, or modulation of inflammatory tumor microenvironment

## Notable Peptides Referenced in Literature

| Peptide | Target | Mechanism | Application |
|---------|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **GE11** (YHWYGYTPQNVI) | EGFR | Binds EGFR with high affinity; screened via phage display | Drug delivery, liposomal targeting, exosome-based therapy |
| **p28** (azurin fragment) | p53 | Stabilizes p53, inhibits proteasomal degradation | Direct antitumor activity; Phase I clinical trials |
| **NKTP-3** | NRP1 + KRASG12D | Dual-targeting cyclic D-peptide | Xenograft and primary lung cancer models |

## Mechanisms of Action

Peptides act through multiple approaches in lung cancer:

1. **Extracellular target binding** — block receptor activation or ligand binding (e.g., EGFR dimerization inhibition)
2. **Intracellular target modulation** — enter cells and disrupt oncogenic signaling (e.g., p53 stabilization, KRAS inhibition)
3. **Tumor-targeted cargo delivery** — function as homing ligands for nanocarriers, liposomes, exosomes, radionuclides, or cytotoxic drugs
4. **Direct cytotoxicity** — membrane disruption and apoptosis induction in cancer cells
5. **Anti-inflammatory effects** — modulation of tumor microenvironment inflammation

## Significance

- First comprehensive review dedicated specifically to peptide strategies against lung cancer
- Maps the peptide landscape across all major NSCLC driver mutations
- Highlights peptides as a bridge between small-molecule TKIs and antibody-based therapies — combining target specificity with favorable pharmacokinetics
- Positions peptides as potential solutions for drug-resistant lung cancers

## Limitations

- Full-text behind paywall; specific peptide sequences and detailed efficacy data not accessible from abstract alone
- Review article — no new experimental data presented
- Clinical translation of most discussed peptides remains in early stages
