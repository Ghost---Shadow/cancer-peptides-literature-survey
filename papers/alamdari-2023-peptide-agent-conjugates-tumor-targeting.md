## Summary

A review covering anticancer peptides as cytotoxic agents, drug carriers, and vaccine components. Catalogs over 60 approved cationic peptides, 28 clinical trial entries, and 12 FDA/EMA-approved ACPs. Discusses peptide-drug conjugate (PDC) design principles including homing peptides, linker chemistry, and cytotoxic payloads. Highlights lung cancer applications including DTX-P7, ANG1005, and CIGB-300.

## PDC Design Framework

```
[Homing Peptide] — [Linker] — [Cytotoxic Payload]
   targeting        cleavable      drug/toxin
```

Three essential components:
1. **Tissue-specific peptides**: Target overexpressed receptors on cancer cells
2. **Linkers**: Cleavable (enzyme, pH, redox) or stable connections
3. **Cytotoxic drugs**: Chemotherapeutics, radionuclides, toxins

## ACP Classification

### By Structure
- Alpha-helical (amphipathic, membrane-interacting)
- Beta-sheet (defensin-like)
- Random coil
- Cyclic (disulfide-stabilized)

### By Mechanism
- **Molecularly targeted** — bind specific oncoproteins
- **Binding/guiding** — homing peptides that deliver payloads
- **Cell-stimulating** — peptide vaccines activating immune responses

## Key Conjugates for Lung Cancer

| Conjugate | Target | Payload | Key Result |
|-----------|--------|---------|------------|
| **DTX-P7** | Hsp90 (overexpressed in NSCLC) | Docetaxel | Induces unfolded protein response → apoptosis; better tumor suppression than DTX alone |
| **ANG1005** | LRP1 (brain + lung) | 3x Paclitaxel | Increased survival in lung carcinoma mice; bypasses P-glycoprotein efflux |
| **CIGB-300** | Casein kinase 2 | — (direct inhibitor) | IC50 ~30 µM in NSCLC; reduces angiogenesis |

## Stability Enhancement Strategies

| Strategy | Benefit |
|----------|---------|
| Cyclization | Protease resistance, conformational stability |
| D-amino acid substitution | Protease resistance |
| Non-natural amino acids | Modified pharmacokinetics |
| PEGylation | Extended half-life, reduced clearance |
| Polymer conjugation | Improved bioavailability |

## Significance

- Comprehensive overview of the PDC landscape from preclinical to FDA-approved
- Maps 28 peptide-based clinical trials across multiple cancer types
- Provides design principles for PDC engineering (homing + linker + payload)
- Highlights DTX-P7 and ANG1005 as lung cancer-relevant conjugates

## Limitations

- Review — no new experimental data
- Published 2023, some clinical trial data may be outdated
- Limited mechanistic depth on linker chemistry
- Lung cancer coverage is secondary to broader oncology focus
