# An Orally Available Grafted Peptide Targeting Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor Dimers Reduces Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer Tumors in Mouse Models

## Summary

This study demonstrates that SFTI-G5, a grafted cyclic peptide targeting HER2 to inhibit EGFR:HER2 and HER2:HER3 dimerization, is orally bioavailable and reduces NSCLC tumors in mouse models. SFTI-G5 uses the stable SFTI-1 (sunflower trypsin inhibitor) scaffold with grafted pharmacophore elements that bind HER2 Domain IV, preventing receptor heterodimerization. The peptide shows sub-micromolar IC50 in NSCLC cell lines while sparing normal cells.

## Peptide Design — SFTI-1 Scaffold Grafting

### SFTI-G5 Structure
- **Scaffold**: SFTI-1 — a 14-amino acid disulfide-stabilized bicyclic peptide from sunflower seeds
- **Sequence**: Cyclo(C(S-X)RIPPR(S-X)CFPDDF) where X = Anapa (3-amino-3-(1-naphthyl)propionic acid)
- **Key feature**: Non-natural amino acid (Anapa) grafted into the SFTI framework
- **Properties**: Thermostable, protease-resistant, non-immunogenic, orally available

### Design Rationale
1. SFTI-1 scaffold provides inherent stability (disulfide bridge + cyclic backbone)
2. Grafting pharmacophore elements onto loops creates target specificity
3. Cyclic peptide resists proteolytic degradation → enables oral delivery
4. Small size (~1.6 kDa) compared to antibodies (~150 kDa)

## Mechanism of Action

1. **HER2 binding**: SFTI-G5 binds HER2 extracellular Domain IV
2. **Dimerization blockade**: Competitively prevents EGFR:HER2 and HER2:HER3 heterodimerization
3. **Signaling inhibition**: Blocks HER2 kinase phosphorylation and downstream Akt signaling
4. **Cancer cell death**: Inhibits proliferation selectively in HER2-overexpressing cells

## Key Results

### In Vitro Antiproliferative Activity (IC50)

| Cell Line | Type | IC50 (µM) |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| Calu-3 | NSCLC (HER2+) | **0.073** |
| A549 | NSCLC (HER2+) | **0.369** |
| BT-474 | Breast (HER2+) | 0.280 |
| MCF-7 | Breast (HER2−) | >20 |
| MCF-10A | Normal breast | >40 |
| HLF | Normal lung fibroblast | >100 |

**Selectivity**: >500-fold difference between cancer and normal cells.

### In Vivo NSCLC Efficacy

**Xenograft Model (Calu-3)**:
- SFTI-G5 at 8 mg/kg intratumoral, 3x/week
- Significant tumor growth delay (p < 0.001 vs control at Day 21)
- No body weight changes

**Experimental Metastasis Model (A549)**:
- SFTI-G5 at 6 mg/kg IV, 2x/week
- Decreased tumor growth rate vs vehicle control
- Reduced tumor burden on histopathology

### Oral Bioavailability (2025 Study)
- Demonstrated oral delivery of SFTI-G5 in NSCLC mouse models
- Tumor growth inhibition via oral route confirmed
- Cyclic SFTI scaffold confers protease resistance enabling GI survival

## Significance

- **First orally available peptide** targeting EGFR family dimerization in NSCLC
- Addresses a critical drug delivery challenge — most peptides require injection
- Targets the dimerization interface rather than the kinase domain — distinct mechanism from TKIs (erlotinib, osimertinib)
- May circumvent TKI resistance (T790M, C797S) since it targets extracellular domain
- SFTI scaffold grafting is a generalizable platform for creating stable, oral peptide drugs
- Sub-micromolar potency competitive with small-molecule inhibitors

## Limitations

- Full 2025 paper behind paywall — detailed oral PK data not extracted
- Intratumoral delivery used in some models (not systemic)
- HER2 expression varies across NSCLC subtypes — patient selection needed
- Long-term in vivo safety and chronic dosing not reported
- Clinical translation timeline unclear
